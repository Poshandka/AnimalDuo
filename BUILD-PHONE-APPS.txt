Animal Crossing phone app coverage and controls, 2026-11-03.
Game 3.0.3; unsigned generic arm64 application 3.0.52; minimum iOS/iPadOS 26.

The player reported incomplete DIY, Miles and My Designs backgrounds after
adaptive scene expansion, old 2D controls instead of the desired 3D default,
and companion busy scrims stopping at the fitted centre instead of covering
the physical sides. A separate reported device crash remains unlocated.

The registered main screen override now derives both axes from the authored
scale: wide displays retain reference height, tall displays retain width.
Retained current matrices are never used as the scale source. Inline keyboards
share the guarded derivation and retain lower-edge docking only on taller
screens. Native HID and gesture coordinates invert the expanded axis.

The verified full-screen P_Tex_00/P_Base_00 and background panes extend fresh
draw constants and active repeated UVs on the required axis, preserving pattern
density. Exact names, reference dimensions, centred horizontal origin, a
matching actual projection and fresh uniform dimensions are required. Local,
partial, square, orthographic and already expanded constants are excluded.
The circular transition retains its separate hierarchy guards and extends each
outer curtain edge on wide targets while keeping the inner aperture boundary,
authored pane transforms and UVs fixed. A copied-island entry reproduced the
previous wide edge strips before this final correction.

Companion full-body rectangular input-blocking scrims keep manifest paint
order but use the physical body clip. Foreground cards retain their fitted
content transform; the footer and native control views stay separate. Physical
controller layout itself is not redesigned in this release. The 3D migration
runs once when production controls are created for a full-layout profile:
absent/old-2D preferences upgrade, previews do not consume migration, and
explicit native-touch mode plus later layout choices remain available.

The owned adapter runs 10,642 ARM64 checks. Native UIKit checks include 36
mask pixel/input cases, 26,524 panel, 32,868 rail, 1,012 ordinary-phone, 560
handoff, 1,248 geometry, 472 legacy-surface/migration, 126 folding/input and
13 startup assertions. Six preview assertions replay historical module data
and owned art in the current hierarchy. Empty synthetic fixtures are included;
raw images, art and snapshots are not. Actual native simulator evidence from
older releases remains historical; no new physical acceptance is inferred.

Actual Mac gameplay uses a disposable island copy. Three phone apps and the
design editor are captured at stock, tall (678/466) and wide (874/402) aspects.
The wide clock/date/minimap/NookPhone are visible. Twenty additional cycles
open and close the three apps 60 times and return to free play without an
observed crash. Final post-close footprint samples range 1959.3..1963.9 MiB;
small residual drift remains and no long-session plateau is established; this is finite sampling, not hours-long acceptance
or a performance gain. Original save fingerprints remain identical and cloud
storage is disabled. Raw captures are before native presentation, not phone
screenshots or a direct-drawing acceptance test.

Host, Metal, ThreadSanitizer, Python and documentation/environment validation,
Release iOS build, module-byte verification and full local game regression are
recorded in the matching validation folder. The regression compares 9,766
shaders/interfaces and startup routes; busy flags and finite frame/memory
observations are retained. No speedup is inferred from that comparison.

Read-only diagnostics have not correlated the player's reported crash with a
matching system/process report. Available Duo logs end in normal background
handling without a host crash marker; older resource reports do not identify
this incident. The connected iPad runs an older app, and the ordinary phone's
developer disk mount failed. Exact device/time/preceding operation remain
needed. This release does not claim to fix that incident, the prior room-load
stall, frame-time spikes or reverse-wide cross-fold absolute heading.
