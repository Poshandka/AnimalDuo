Animal Crossing larger persistent companion controls, 2026-11-03.
Game 3.0.3; unsigned generic arm64 application 3.0.51; minimum iOS/iPadOS 26.

The player requested larger portrait controls, higher plus/minus and controls
that remain visible during companion operations. The previous whole-panel
busy suppression predated the shared side frames. Gesture, local-operation and
native-menu notifications could still hide controls and reset held game input.

Reserved portrait controls now remain visible and interactive through companion
operations and upper-game typing/drawing. The bridge accepts the same activity
callbacks without the obsolete state and restoration timer. Inactive
presentation, a physical controller and explicit Direct touch retain their
existing hide policy. Landscape keyboard/drawing lifetime and auto fade remain.

The shared side columns increase from 14% (60..110 points) to 16% (68..128 points).
Default controls grow approximately 15% in native portrait surfaces. The fitted
middle body, drawn paths and inverse interaction coordinates use the same
partition. Plus/minus occupy the middle row between upper/lower controls; L3/R3
move to the lower function row. Short surfaces protect the complete 44-point
menu targets from adjacent groups. Whole default groups stay centred, custom
scaling and offsets remain confined, and the footer stays at the panel bottom.
Main game size, equal Duo halves and ordinary-phone landscape remain unchanged.

The UIKit matrix passes 26,524 panel assertions across ninety default/scaled/
extreme-offset poses. Actual painted paths and hit regions are compared with
the Swift renderer's middle frame. Real A remains held throughout overlapping
touch/local/native operation notifications and releases normally. Native-size
default controls grow at least 10%, and both menu keys move up. The preceding
32,868 rail, 1,012 ordinary-phone, 560 handoff, 1,248 geometry/hosting, 466 legacy
surface, 126 folding/input and 13 startup checks pass. C geometry checks also
protect menu hit targets from adjacent face/D-pad/stick extents.

Six preview assertions pass. Pictures replay historical owned module state and
original art in the current native hierarchy. They are not live gameplay or
physical-device captures; raw images/art/snapshots are excluded from source.
Native simulator rotation evidence from 3.0.50 remains historical. This release
does not claim another physical-device or native-simulator acceptance run.

Complete host, Metal, ThreadSanitizer, tools (310, sixteen skipped), iOS (42),
graphics Python (71, five skipped) and documentation/environment checks pass.
The unchanged adapter passes 490 ARM64 checks and material tests compare 1,024
Metal results. Full local game regression compares all 9,766 shaders/interfaces
and consecutive 90-second baseline/candidate routes. Busy flags are retained;
these finite observations are not performance improvement or hours-long proofs.

Only companion_layout.h, control_presentation.h, the companion C tests and iOS
front-end visibility policy change relative to 3.0.50, plus their documentation.
The view/orientation routines are byte-identical. DmGamepad and Swift sources,
guest rewrites, shaders, saves, cloud code/paths and persistent resources remain.
The known reverse-wide cross-fold heading change, intermittent room-loading
stall and unexplained local frame-time spikes remain open.
