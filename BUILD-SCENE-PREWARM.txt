Limits
Measurements are from the local M4 with an existing system Metal cache. Physical iPad/iPhone/M2, first installation, OS cache eviction, every weather/season, shops, museum and every possible room/model combination are not established by these routes. One-second footprint samples, retained in five-second batches, can miss short allocation peaks; process footprint includes test instrumentation. Actual presented timestamps and gaps, startup prepare and game-thread compilation are distinct evidence. The optimization moves known compilation before gameplay. Unlisted combinations can still compile when first used.
